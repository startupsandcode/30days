About Amara

Dr. Amara Okafor spent a decade building machine-learning systems at two Fortune 100 companies before asking a harder question: what does responsible innovation actually look like inside a company? She now advises boards and governments on AI governance, and delivers keynotes that turn abstract ethics into Monday-morning action.

Her book "Machines With Manners" (2024) spent 11 weeks on the business bestseller list, and her TED talk on "The Dignity of Work in an Automated Age" has been viewed over 8 million times.
