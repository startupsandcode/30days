The thriller the tech world can't put down

Mara Kessler hasn't shipped code in four years — not since the incident. But when her old co-founder dies the night before launch, the board begs her to do one last thing: read the repository.

What she finds in the commit history is a confession written in diffs, a murder plotted in pull requests, and a killer who signs their work.

About the Author

She writes the bugs she knows.

✒️

Elena Voss

Elena Voss spent eleven years as a backend engineer before trading stack traces for story arcs. She led infrastructure at two startups, survived three rewrites, and once found a production bug by reading a haiku in a commit message — an experience that became this novel.

Her short fiction has appeared in Ellery Queen's Mystery Magazine and The Dark. The Long Debug is her debut novel, written mostly between midnight and 2am, which she insists is when both code and prose are most honest.

She lives in Portland with a dog named Regex and a sourdough starter named Segfault.
