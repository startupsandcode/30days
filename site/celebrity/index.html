NOVA REYES

The voice of a generation. 40 million monthly listeners, 3 sold-out world tours, and a smile that broke the internet twice.

Take the Quiz 🎤 Her Story

The Girl Behind the Stardust

Born Elena Reyes in San Juan, Puerto Rico, Nova taught herself piano on a keyboard with two broken keys — and never looked back.

🎹 The Early Years

Nova grew up above her abuela's bakery, writing songs between batches of pan de agua. At 14 she uploaded a bedroom cover of a classic bolero to the internet — and woke up to two million views.

Hometown: San Juan, Puerto Rico · Born: March 14, 2001

🌟 The Nova Sound

Reggaetón rhythms, orchestral pop, and lyrics in Spanglish that feel like a diary entry. Critics call it "arena pop with a heartbeat." Fans just call it Nova.

Signature song: "Electric Mar" · Fandom name: The Constellation ✨

💜 Off the Stage

She runs the Reyes Foundation, funding music programs in 30 Puerto Rican schools, and famously answers fan mail by hand every Sunday morning.

Foundation: Reyes Foundation · Pets: Two rescue dogs, Sol y Luna

🏆 By the Numbers

12 Grammy nominations, 4 wins. Her 2023 "Prisma World Tour" played 58 cities across 5 continents — including a record 4 nights at San Juan's Coliseo.

Monthly listeners: 40M · Biggest crowd: 92,000 in Mexico City

Career Timeline

From bedroom covers to stadium lights — every milestone, in order.

2019

🎤 Breakout: "Midnight en la Isla"

Her debut single drops independently and hits #1 in 12 Latin American countries. Nova signs with Lumen Records three months later — at 18.

2020

💿 Debut EP "Luces"

A six-track EP recorded in her childhood bedroom studio. Goes platinum in 9 months despite zero radio play — pure streaming power.

2021

🌊 Album "Electric Mar" + First Grammy

Her debut full album sells 2M copies in its first week. Wins Best New Artist at the Grammys and performs the closing number.

2022

🎬 Film Debut & Second Album

Stars in the musical film "Constelación" and releases "Neón Alma," featuring the smash duet with fellow icon Marco Vela.

2024

🏟️ The Prisma World Tour

58 cities, 5 continents, 92,000 fans in Mexico City. The tour documentary "Nova: Prisma" becomes the most-streamed music film of the year.

2026

✨ "Supernova" Era Begins

Her third album "Supernova" drops this spring with a surprise stadium show in San Juan — free for 20,000 local fans.
