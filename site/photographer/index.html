About Maya

I'm Maya Chen — a photographer who believes the best portraits happen between poses. My work has appeared in Kinfolk, California Sunday, and three group shows at the SFMOMA Artists Gallery.

When I'm not shooting, I'm teaching a free weekend photo workshop for teens in the Mission, or developing film in a closet I refuse to call a darkroom.
